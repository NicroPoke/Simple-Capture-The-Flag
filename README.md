## Directions
- May need to run "./gradlew IntellijRuns" in the terminal
- May need to reload gradle projects by pressing refresh button in Gradle of right side bar of IntelliJ Idea IDE
## How it works
- Place a Red Point and a Blue Point from the new Capture The Flag creative tab.
- Players join vanilla scoreboard teams (/team with the red or blue color) - the mod uses team colors, no extra accounts or setup.
- A player from the opposing team who enters the point's radius starts capturing it: a boss bar with a countdown appears (default 5 minutes, adjustable).
- Each additional attacker on the point speeds up the capture (2 players = x2, 3 = x3 …).
- A defender standing on the point freezes the capture at its current progress until the defenders leave.
- When the timer runs out, the point switches to the capturing team's color - flag model, beam and all.
